Return & Refund Policy – GourmetGrove

Because chocolates are perishable, we only accept returns or issue refunds under specific conditions:

1. Eligible Returns/Refunds

  • Wrong product received
  • Damaged or melted chocolates on arrival
  • Missing items from your order
  • Package lost in transit

Note: Requests must be made within 48 hours of delivery with photo/video evidence.

2. Non-Refundable Cases

  • Change of mind or taste preference
  • Opened or partially consumed chocolates
  • Delay due to courier service beyond our control

3. Process

  1. Email support@themunchloom.com with your order ID, description, and proof.
  2. Our team will verify and arrange a replacement or refund to the original payment method within 5–7 working days (if approved).
Important: Please ensure you provide clear photos or videos of any damaged items to help us process your request quickly.

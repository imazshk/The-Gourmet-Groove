Shipping Policy – GourmetGrove

1. General

We deliver chocolates, candies, and confections across India. Our goal is to get your treats to you quickly and in perfect condition.

2. Processing & Dispatch

  • Orders placed before 2 PM are processed the same day (Mon–Sat).
  • Orders placed on Sundays or holidays are processed the next working day.
  • Delivery time depends on your location (2–7 working days on average).

3. Shipping Fees

  • Free shipping for orders above ₹999.
  • Standard delivery charges apply otherwise, based on location.

4. Unserviceable Areas

If we cannot deliver to your pin code, you'll be notified before checkout.

5. Damaged Parcels

If your package arrives melted, damaged, or tampered with, please refuse delivery and contact us immediately.
